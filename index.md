
I am an associate professor in the [Department of Intelligent Systems](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/about/schools/tshd/departments/dis), part of the [Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence research center](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/about/schools/tshd/departments/dca) at Tilburg University.

I studied mathematics at Koç University and did my PhD in the discrete algebra and geometry group at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), defending in January 2012. My thesis generalised a classical combinatorial theorem of Erdős, Ko and Rado on families of intersecting sets, using algebraic graph theory and combinatorics. I then worked for four years in banking and consultancy as a quantitative analyst and data analyst, mostly with financial and trade data. In 2016 I returned to academia as a postdoc in the System Architecture and Networking group at TU/e, working on computational trust, machine learning and sensor data.

### Research

My research is about network analysis and learning on graph-structured data. I study how the structure of a graph, both its topological and spectral properties, relates to the learning process. This includes how networks evolve, how to detect key substructures and explainability, and how to make predictions on graphs. I am particularly interested in how relationships, communication, similarity and information exchange, captured in the edges, shape network structure, influence motif formation and affect learning. My main applications are brain networks and electrical grids.

### Projects with a social aim

Data science projects with a social aspect motivate me. Several of my projects use AI to create a more inclusive and accessible world:
- **Ilustre** (ICAI lab): sustainable water management and energy transition in Curaçao.
- **ICON project** (Zero Poverty Lab): linking brain networks and poverty.
- **Child Growth Monitor** (Zero Hunger Lab): detecting malnutrition in children from images.
