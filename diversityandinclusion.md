## Diversity and Inclusion

Diversity, equity and inclusion are themes that I feel very strongly about in life, education and in the working environment. This commitment drives my involvement in various initiatives aimed at promoting these principles. Below are some of the things I have been part of, with notes on what others might try in their own setting.

**Working across institutions.** Since 2021 I have represented our department in the [IPN EDI working group](https://ict-research.nl/edi-working-group/), and I have co-chaired it since September 2025. Its aim is to advance equity and inclusion across the Dutch ICT research community. *Idea: Here is a list of [members](https://ict-research.nl/organisation-edi-working-group/), you can spot who is representing your department/university;  feel free to reach out to them and share your thoughts and concerns*

**Inside the school.** From 2022 until September 2026 I served on the Diversity and Inclusion Committee of the [Tilburg School of Humanities and Digital Sciences](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/nl/over/schools/tshd). *Idea: Find out what your institution’s inclusion strategy says, and who is responsible for it. Where it is vague, suggest one specific step.*

**Bringing people together.** In June 2023 I organized the workshop "[Science and AI in Support of Diversity and Inclusion](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/about/schools/tshd/departments/dca/science-and-ai-support-diversity-and-inclusion)", showing how science can contribute to a more inclusive society. Participants came from philosophy, cultural studies, law, communication and cognition, and from other universities. *Idea: Idea: Inclusion comes up in every discipline. Bring different fields together to compare what each one does and learn from it.*

**Diversity tracks and talks.** I co-chaired the [Diversity track](https://ictopen.nl/track-diversity-in-ict) at [ICT.OPEN](https://ictopen.nl/) in [2024/2025], and I sit on the scientific committee of [Hack4Her](https://hack4her.github.io/2024_event.html). At Hack4Her in 2024 I gave a talk, "Using AI to Support Accessibility for All", on how research topics, methods and frameworks can address accessibility.

**Writing it down.** With colleagues from our department I co-wrote ["AI in Support of Diversity and Inclusion"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09534). It collects research lines where AI helps: gender bias in language, representation of people in images, bias quantification and mitigation, LGBTQ+ disinformation across Europe, more diverse datasets, and sign-language translation for deaf and hard of hearing people. I wanted inclusion to be treated in three places: in our working environment, in the classroom, and in what our research produces.

I like to tackle the diversity issue in different ways, really as an experience in the workplace for us, in the classroom for students, but also with how our work contributes to it. That is why I wanted to have this paper put together.

If this is a priority for you too and you'd like to do something together, get in touch please.
