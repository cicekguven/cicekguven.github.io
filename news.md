## Recent events

I served as a jury member in the PDEng defense of Knarik Sahakyan (JADS) on 25 September 2026.

Our paper "Probabilistic Residual Load Modelling with Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks and Mondrian Conformal Calibration", first authored by Ugochukwu Orji, was presented at the [International Conference on Smart Energy Systems and Technologies (SEST) 2026](https://www.sest2026.com/).

Our paper "A Grid-Aware Spatio-Temporal Framework for Probabilistic Load Forecasting with Mondrian Conformal Calibration" is accepted at [IEEE PES ISGT Europe 2026](https://ieee-isgt-europe.org/).

Our paper "Set Classification with Probabilistic Learning Vector Quantization on the Grassmann Manifold" is accepted at [IJCNN 2026](https://attend.ieee.org/wcci-2026/ijcnn-2026-topics/).

I was a member of the poster and demo jury of [NWO ICT.OPEN 2026](https://ictopen.nl/) and of the poster jury of [Alice & Eve 2026](https://alice-and-eve.github.io/2026/).

I served as a jury member in the PDEng defense of Yue Pan (JADS) on 31 March 2026.

## Past

Hezha Mohammedkhan [defended](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/current/events/phd-defense-hezha-mohammedkhan) her PhD on 23 March 2026.

Since January 2026, I chair the informatics board of the [Lorentz Center](https://www.lorentzcenter.nl/home.html).

Since Fall 2025, I co-chair the [Equity, Diversity and Inclusion working group of IPN](https://ict-research.nl/edi-wg/).

I was a member of the jury for the ROBUST (LTP) awards, the Interdisciplinary Research Award and the Lab Collaboration Award, presented at ICAI day 2025.

We released the ILUSTRE Lab consortium visit report, [Exploring Sustainable Water Management and Energy Transition in Curaçao](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/exploring-sustainable-water-management-and-energy-transition-in-c/).

Our paper [Harnessing Firm Similarities with Graph Neural Networks for Superior Stock Predictions](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-82427-2_19) is published.

Our paper "Grid-Aware Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks for Multi-Horizon Load Forecasting" was presented at the [14th DACH+ Conference on Energy Informatics](https://energy-informatics2025.org/). It is first authored by Ugochukwu Orji, a PhD candidate I supervise.

Our paper "Determining graphlet explanations for machine learning on graphs" is published in PLOS Complex Systems. It is first authored by Bettina Soós, a PhD candidate I supervise.

I was appointed Associate Professor in the Department of Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence at Tilburg University on 1 August 2025.

I presented at the [International Conference on Complex Systems in France (FRCCS 2025)](https://iutdijon.u-bourgogne.fr/ccs-france/).

The [ARAN: Age-Restricted Anonymized Dataset of Images and Measurements of Children](https://dataverse.nl/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.34894/FWYPYC) is released. We believe it will contribute to research on learning children's body measurements from 2D images, with a lot of potential for healthcare applications.

I chaired the Learning on Graphs session on 12 June at the [4th AIM Cluster Event](https://aimath.nl/index.php/2025/03/13/4th-aim-cluster-event-tilburg/).
